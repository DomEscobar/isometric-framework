# Compose the whole environment

Use for a complete environment or substantial visual refinement. A focused repair
uses only the affected relationships and views. Derive decisions from the approved
brief; these examples do not prescribe a biome, asset count, palette or layout.

## Plan from scene to area to contact

Before expanding the asset pack, establish three connected scales:

| Scale | Decide | Inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whole scene | Focal areas, silhouette, readable routes, relative sizes, dense and quiet space | Overview and actual playing camera; the actor and useful destinations remain legible |
| Area | Which objects and surfaces belong together, their use, placement rhythm and relation to adjacent areas | A complete grouping and its boundary to the next area, rather than isolated assets |
| Contact | How each relevant object/material pair meets: foundation/paving, rock/soil, bank/water, root/grass | Ground-only and dressed views with the actor, including the weakest visible join |

Put intended experience and visual outcomes in the existing brief. Derive area
choices and asset families in existing host production data; use the host's layout
IDs where available. Do not add another contract or duplicate coordinates. For
each important area, briefly identify its role, adjoining areas, shared materials,
needed forms/variants and contacts. This reasoning precedes the asset shopping list.

For fixed geometry, compose inside its reserved routes and footprints. For flexible
geometry, reconcile the concept with traversal before freezing acceptance. A rough
whole-scene blockout belongs to the layout stage; it need not be another generated
image. A new image is useful only when it resolves a real visual decision within
the authorized budget.

## Derive families from relationships

| Area example | Connected design | Failure and repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Street frontage | Entrances meet worn approaches; related buildings share construction while their roles shape frontage; a clear route crosses quieter paving | Evenly spaced identical houses float beside a road: revise grouping, frontage roles and entrance connections before adding props |
| Courtyard | Main entrance or feature organizes open space; furniture groups serve reachable activities; walls, paving and thresholds share scale and light | Many unrelated props fill every gap: remove competing detail, recover useful space and regroup around the intended activity |
| Rocky shore | Rock masses frame the water; exposed bases, wet edges and sediment connect the surfaces; access remains readable | Unique rocks sit as isolated stickers: compose their supporting ground and shared edge treatment, then inspect the whole bank |

Choose repetition deliberately. One tree species in an avenue, matching windows,
regular paving or a restrained minimalist scene can be correct. Variation may be
needed in silhouette, frontage, wear, edge treatment, grouping or spacing; it does
not always mean another object type. Many unique assets can still make a confused
scene. Renamed duplicates, arbitrary tint/scale changes and random scattering do
not establish useful variety. Preserve projection, lighting and player-relative
scale when producing real variants.

Reuse accepted family members where they fit. Produce the missing form or material
connection that a specific area needs. Do not demand a fixed number of species,
buildings or variants, or fill intentional empty space to increase a count.

## Design together, export according to behavior

A connected area is a unit of visual design, not necessarily one image or collider.
Modular art can use compatible ground/contact pieces; composed ground can reserve
joins for separate objects; layered artwork can separate foreground and motion.
Use the chosen technique and the [contact guidance](../../isometric-art-integration/references/grounded-assemblies.md).
An already selected modular, composed or layered approach remains selected;
planning connected areas does not reopen that choice or authorize a hybrid.

When a ground image and later props are separate requests, agree the approach,
base material, footprint and layer ownership first. Each request carries the
relevant shared decisions and accepted visual authority. Render them together
before expanding. Do not remove a necessary contact zone during cutout preparation
or bake traversal/occlusion into one sprite merely to keep the picture together.

The first asset proves generation/decoding feasibility. A representative live area
proves relationships and joins. Neither establishes complete-world quality. Choose
an area exposing the important relationships already requested, then check other
material/area types during expansion instead of repeating an unrelated calibration.

## Review and repair at the scale of the defect

Translate these outcomes into separate visual requirements in the existing
acceptance plan: composition/readability, relationships/appropriate variation,
material/object connections and shared image treatment. Adapt them to the actual
scope. Reuse captures across criteria; add a detail view only when playing-scale
evidence cannot show a required connection. Keep natural-terrain criteria explicit
through the [terrain rubric](../../consistent-tileset-authoring/references/landscape-composition.md#terrain-acceptance-evidence).

Review the complete frame, important areas and their boundaries, then the weakest
contacts. Locate the strongest repeated motif and say whether it serves the design
or distracts. With a style-only reference assess hierarchy, density rhythm and
material language without requiring its object positions. A contact detail cannot
replace the overview, and a pleasing overview cannot waive a failed connection.

Fix composition through grouping, scale relationships and open space; fix an
incoherent area through related forms/materials; fix a contact through its actual
edge/layer/placement. Diagnose a floating base before editing: source pixels,
anchor, scale, placement and depth order can each cause it. Preserve a contact
origin only when measurements confirm it is correct. Extra clutter, palette matching or animation cannot close
those findings. Keep stable finding IDs and recapture affected views after repair;
the existing comparison loop tracks fail/unverified/resolved outcomes.

Tools enforce declared coverage and evidence freshness, not artistic judgment.
No asset count, valid collision map or completed checklist proves harmony. Missing
or inconclusive visual inspection remains unverified.
